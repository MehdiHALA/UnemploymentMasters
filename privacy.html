Privacy Policy

PSN Trophy Bot · last updated 1 November 2026

What the bot stores

  • Your Discord user ID and the server ID where you linked a PSN account.
  • The PSN Online ID and account ID you linked, and that account's public trophy statistics, avatar and recently played games.
  • Which platinum trophies were announced.
  • Server settings chosen by server admins: channels, colours, trophy emojis, the weekly post time and roles.
  • The names and member counts of the servers the bot is in, used only for the developer's admin tools.

In servers with Platinum, also

  • A daily record of trophy totals for linked accounts, kept for 90 days.
  • Race entries and progress, deleted 90 days after a race ends.
  • Monthly starting totals for the monthly standings, replaced each month.
  • Which milestone roles the bot gave to members.

How long

Accounts looked up with /platinums or /compare without being linked are kept for up to 30 days. Use /removepsn to unlink an account; it then stops being updated, and once it is linked in no server its trophy data is deleted within 30 days.

Payments

Platinum purchases are handled by Discord. The bot only learns whether a server has an active Platinum subscription, never payment details.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared. Trophy data is only read from PlayStation Network's public trophy information. This bot is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub or use /support. See also the Terms of Service.
