Privacy Policy

To run per-server PSN trophy leaderboards, the bot stores: your Discord user ID and the server ID where you linked an account; the PSN Online ID and account ID you linked; that account's public trophy statistics, avatar and recently played games; and which platinum trophies were announced.

Accounts looked up with /platinums or /compare without being linked are kept for up to 30 days. Use /removepsn to unlink an account; it then stops being updated. Data is not sold or shared, and is only read from PlayStation Network's public trophy information.

This bot is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment.
